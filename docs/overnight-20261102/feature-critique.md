**Secondary (A):** the 0.2s rise from floor contact to vertical guard is quick. Check it in motion for a snap. It is not a blocker in the stills.

## WINGS: **PASS B**. B is ready to replace A for wing pose, with breathing unproven.

**A**
- **Idle:** the wings are folded tight against the flank. Across all 24 idle frames (0.017–6.018s) they show only as a small dark-red patch on the back, a small fraction of the body's length. That reads as cramped.
- **Transition:** the wings then visibly unfold from that tuck to a large open drape within about 0.25s (0.617 → 0.867s). That is a pose change during the roar entry, not a clean carry-over.

**B**
- **Idle:** the wings are relaxed and half-open. The membrane and finger bones are visible, and the wings cover roughly the rear half of the body side. That is a natural folded-at-rest proportion for this creature.
- **Transition:** the idle wing pose already matches the roar pose, so 0.633–1.384s shows no unfolding. The handoff is clean.

**Breathing:** I can't confirm breathing for either one. At 0.25s sample spacing in low light, neither sheet shows measurable chest or flank movement. Nothing looks frozen, but this needs checking in motion.

**Secondary (B):** the membrane hangs over the hind thigh. Check in motion that the leg doesn't pass through the wing when weight shifts.
