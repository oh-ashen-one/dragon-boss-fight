# Blind pacing round 2 — PASS A, with remaining gaps

Independent Claude Opus 5.5 high. Chronology and implementation notes were withheld. The critic read uniform 2-second timelines, 0.25-second ending sequences and final details from the same Shipping build. It did not watch continuous playback. Mapping revealed afterward: {"A": "revised pacing, contact-corrected death and retained framing", "B": "legacy pacing at the same 650 HP"}.

Scoped comparative pass for damage progress and death readability. Remaining critic issues are a low-health flight stall, ending head overlap, close camera crowding and a thin wing spike near the floor. These are not waived by the fidelity/timing checks. The next controlled revision addresses the low-health flight choice and ending shoulder position without reducing HP.

## Critic report (verbatim)

**Preferred: A**

**A (90.8 s)**
- **Health progress:** The bar is full from 0 to about 20 s, then falls steadily. It is roughly 80% at 30 s, about 65% at 44 s, about 38% at 50 s, about 20% at 54–56 s and about 5% at 64 s. Damage is spread across several visible exchanges instead of coming in one burst.
- **Punish windows:** The face-on head-down window from about 52 to 62 s takes roughly 25% (about 30% down to about 8%). That is a real opening, but not one that decides the fight.
- **Regression / shared defect:** A sliver of health stays on the bar from about 64 s to about 82.9 s. That is roughly 17 s with no progress while the dragon flies and repositions (68–74 s). The bar first reads empty at 83.65 s.
- **Death:** This reads clearly. The dragon tips onto its side between 84.38 and 85.65 s. From 85.65 to 90.64 s it is fully still: head and jaw flat on the floor, wing splayed, body on the ground. A-last.jpg shows believable contact between head and neck and the floor.
- **Defects:**
  - A-last.jpg: the knight stands over the dragon's neck and blocks part of the head.
  - A thin wing spike goes down into the floor at the right.
  - 81.14–81.65 s: a blurry, low-detail dragon leg fills the right half of the frame.
  - 28–32 s: the camera crowds into the dragon's belly and neck.

**B (110.4 s)**
- **Health progress:** The bar shows no readable loss for about 75 s (0–74.0 s are all full). This is despite many close contacts with the weapon raised beside the dragon (16–30 s, 32–46 s). The first visible loss is at about 76 s, and it is only about 90% by 84 s and about 75% by 94 s.
- **Punish windows:** About 70% of the health drains in one head-down window from about 100.2 to 106.75 s (bar about 75% at 100.24 s, about 35% at 105.0 s, about 10% at 106.48 s, empty at 106.75 s). One repeated punish against a static head decides the fight, with no worthwhile openings beforehand.
- **Death:** This does not read as death. From about 99 s to the end the dragon holds the same crouch: head forward, claws planted, wings raised. Its posture during the punish and after death is identical, and there is no fall in the final 10 s. In B-last.jpg the knight stands centred directly in front of the dragon's head, hiding the face. Only the empty bar tells you the dragon died.
- **Camera blocking:**
  - 72.0 s: a bright, washed-out flank fills about 40% of the frame.
  - 74.0 s: a leg fills the frame.
  - 98.0 s: a pale leg fills half the frame.
  - 100.24–104 s: the head and horns crowd the knight.

**Shared problems**
- Both runs open with about 20 s of full health.
- In both, the knight and weapon merge with the dragon's dark head and claws during face-on punishes (A at 52–62 s, B at 100–106 s). Weapon swings are only clear when the club extends sideways against the floor.
- Both endings leave the knight standing over or in front of the dragon's head.

**Limitations:** Samples are 2 s apart (0.25 s at the end), so I can't judge motion, hit feel or anything between samples. Health percentages are read by eye from bar length, not measured in pixels.

**Biggest gap (applies to A):** Remove the 64–83 s stall at low health. The bar should reach empty within 6 s of falling below 5%. The death camera, or where the player stands, must keep the dragon's head unobstructed for the whole of the final 5 s.

**Verdict:** A is clearly better on visible pacing (progress over about 44 s against B's 75 s with no progress followed by a 70% burst) and on the death reading (a clear fall onto its side with the head on the floor, against B's unchanged crouch). A still has a low-health stall and blocked framing at the end.

**PASS A**
