## Blind review: dragon arena, A vs B

### 1. Images inspected
I opened and looked at all 19 images:
- `target-reference-1.png`, `target-reference-2.png`, `target-reference-3.png` (concept art)
- `A-view-01.png` through `A-view-08.png`
- `B-view-01.png` through `B-view-08.png`

### 2. Winner: **A**, by a wide margin

### 3. Verdict: **DO NOT SHIP. BOTH FAIL.**
A is the clear winner. But even A looks like a clean kitbash pass, not a finished boss arena. B fails the brief outright and has many broken-looking views.

---

### Version A, judged on its own
**What works**
- It reads as a secular military fortress. You see a crenellated curtain wall, square towers, machicolations, a gatehouse with a portcullis and iron strap bands with rivets. There is no church language anywhere (views 1, 2, 7, 8).
- The combat floor is large and empty. In view 1 the dragon and the knight both have clean silhouettes, and the dragon's shadow grounds it.
- The murals are good, especially up close (views 3–6). The masonry block relief shows through the paint, the fading is believable, the riveted iron band runs across them and they cover four different subjects: a dragon over an army, a crown and banner, chained dragons with a heraldic shield, and an astrolabe or star chart. This is the closest either version gets to the target art.

**Defects**
- **Floor (views 1, 2, 8):** A flat, pale-grey surface that looks like concrete. It has no paving pattern, joints, wet sheen or grime. It is the brightest large surface in the wide shots and looks unfinished next to the cobbles in the target art.
- **Mural lighting (views 1, 2):** From a distance, each mural is lit by a tight, hot orange pool with a hard edge. They read as projected glowing discs, like lit display panels, rather than old paint seen under torchlight. In view 2 all three sit on one wall run; the left wall and the gate wall have none visible.
- **Floating island (view 2):** The fortress is a box sitting on a small rock chunk in a flat blue-grey void. There is no terrain, cliff, fog or distant skyline. It also shows that the layout is a simple rectangle built from repeated modules.
- **Repetition (views 1, 8):** The same crenellation and machicolation module repeats along every wall, and the stone texture tiling is visible on the long walls in view 8.
- **Gate (view 7):** The portcullis is plain, uniformly black bars with no iron texture or rust. It looks pasted onto the arch rather than set into a groove. There is also a bright white-blue sliver at the top of the left tower (around x 400–420), which looks like a light leak or a stray piece of geometry.
- **Props too small (views 1, 8):** The braziers and debris piles are tiny against the walls, and the debris piles in view 1 read as random clutter.
- **Lighting (views 1, 2, 7, 8):** Flat moonlight with almost no atmosphere: no fog, no volumetric light and weak rim light. It is not ominous. The dragon is only moderately distinct from the dark wall behind it in view 1.

### Version B, judged on its own
**Fails the brief.** This is plainly a ruined cathedral:
- a rose-window church front (view 2)
- traceried lancet windows (view 7)
- a robed saint or Madonna statue (views 4, 6)
- shrine panels carved with saints (views 1, 8)
- candle chandeliers (views 1, 8)

**No murals are visible in any of the eight views,** including the four perimeter close-ups (3–6).

**Technical and visual defects**
- **View 1:** A huge dark block hangs over the court at the top centre. The dragon nearly vanishes against the dark gothic backdrop. Foreground stone blocks crowd both edges of the frame, and pale blue moonlight patches on the floor look like light leaks.
- **View 2:** Giant smooth arch-rib or vault shells with a flat blue-grey surface look like placeholders. The overall layout is a chaotic pile of mismatched pieces, and most of the floor is hidden.
- **Views 3, 5, 6:** Big blobby rock meshes with smeared, stretched textures and glowing red crack lines clip into gothic columns. They are hard to read and look low quality. View 5 adds a bare tree branch.
- **View 7:** A black rectangular slab floats on top of the tracery window.
- **View 8:** The lighting splits hard down the middle, with half the arcade lit orange and half lit blue-white. Black boxes near the stairs look untextured, a chandelier's shadow is projected on the wall, and a shrine panel in the foreground blocks part of the view.
- **Overall:** The moon, sky and amount of detail are more dramatic than A's, but the extra assets come at the cost of coherence, a readable floor and the brief itself.

### 5. What A needs before it could ship
- A real paving material on the floor.
- Wider, softer, flickering light on the murals, plus more murals on the other walls.
- Terrain or cliffs and fog around the island, and a distant skyline.
- Iron material on the portcullis, and the light leak at the top of the gate tower fixed.
- Larger braziers, plus banners or other features to break up the repeated wall modules.
- Moody, volumetric light that still keeps the dragon readable.

### 6. Limits of this review
- These are eight 1280×720 still frames, which look like editor viewport captures. Post-processing, the gameplay camera and the HUD may differ in the actual game.
- The close-ups (3–6) only show what the camera was pointed at. B may have murals somewhere outside these frames, but none are visible here.
- I judged still images only. I did not see any motion, animation, gameplay or audio.
