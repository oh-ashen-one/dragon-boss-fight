# Fortress asset and placement plan — first pass

Reference direction: player/reverse concepts in Local/Fortress20261103/References. Current native arena keeps a 36 m combat radius, with court footprint approximately 96 x 88 m and raised peripheral galleries. Preserve that clearance while replacing cathedral/apse vocabulary.

## Small modular set
- FortressGatehouse: central military opening, 20 m nominal height. First Tripo job is the representative test; import and inspect before further paid 3D jobs. Raw H3.1 mesh has ~1.81M triangles; make an editable 150k base and native 60k/15k LODs.
- MuralWallBay: broad flat recessed panel, stone frame, battlements; repeats around perimeter with four unique mural materials. No baked mural in the mesh reference.
- Tower/Buttress: square damaged military mass at corners/ends; reuse approved stone geometry when it fits.
- IronGallery: modular supports/railings, mainly on one side; low collision cost. Existing suitable braziers/paving/rubble remain supporting assets.

## Mural assignments
- Dragon siege: west lateral wall, ochre/iron-red history panel.
- Fallen banners: approach/reverse wall, defeated secular army.
- Chained wyrm: opposite lateral wall, military heraldry.
- Astronomical war-chart: east lateral wall, faded scientific/celestial markings.

## Assembly constraints
- Large gate on one end, lower approach gate on reverse end; no nave/apse or worship axis.
- Front walls beyond the 36 m combat disk plus body/camera safety margin; galleries remain peripheral.
- Mural roughness follows masonry, no emissive glow or opaque floating posters.
- Keep raw Tripo/editable files private; versioned native assets through existing import_polish_environment pipeline.
- Reference images are targets only. Final map must be assessed through actual gameplay camera and every-move replay.
