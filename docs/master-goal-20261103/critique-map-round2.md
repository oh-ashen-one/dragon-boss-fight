# Blind A/B critique: dragon boss arena, fortress/war-court brief

## 1. Images inspected
I opened and viewed all 19 images with the Read tool:
- **A-view-01** through **A-view-08**
- **B-view-01** through **B-view-08**
- **target-reference-1**, **target-reference-2** and **target-reference-3**

I read nothing else in the folder.

## 2. Winner: **B**, by a wide margin

## 3. Verdict: **DO NOT SHIP. BOTH FAIL.**
B is clearly better than A, but it is still not good enough to ship as shown. A fails the brief outright.

---

## Version A, assessed on its own
**It breaks the brief's main rule: this is a cathedral.** It has rose windows and church tracery (view 2, top left; view 7), statues of praying nuns and saints (views 4, 6, 8), carved stone panels of saints (views 1, 8) and candle chandeliers hanging in the open air (view 1). It reads as a ruined abbey, not a military fortress or war-court.

- **View 1:** A large dark rectangular mass hangs over the court at top centre with nothing holding it up. Chandeliers hang from the open sky. The floor is a flat dark plane crossed by hard blue-white light shapes that look like light leaking through gaps. The dragon is readable. The player is small but readable.
- **View 2:** The overview shows huge, plain grey vault-rib shells that look like unfinished blockout geometry. Buildings are kitbashed together with no clear logic. A flat grey ground plane runs out into empty space past the edges.
- **Views 3, 5 and 6:** Close-ups show blobby, low-detail rock meshes with smeared or stretched texturing and orange glowing cracks. Rock pushes through the Gothic columns. Behind the arches is a black void.
- **View 7:** A dark box clips through the top of the tower crown.
- **View 8:** There is a hard lighting seam across one continuous arcade: warm orange on the left, cold moonlight on the right. The stair plinths and platforms are pure black and look untextured. A chandelier's shadow falls on the sky opening.
- **Murals:** none visible in any A view.

## Version B, assessed on its own
The style is right: a secular castle with crenellations, machicolations, arrow slits, a portcullis and iron bands with studs. The masonry on the walls is decent. The overall scene still falls short:

- **View 1:**
  - The floor is a flat, featureless grey surface with no visible paving, nothing like the flagstones in the targets.
  - Bright white slash-shaped patches on the floor look like light leaks.
  - Thin dark horizontal lines cross the tower on the right, like wires or seams.
  - From this distance the mural is a glowing orange disc that looks lit from behind, not like aged paint.
  - The walls sit straight on the floor with no base or plinth.
  - On the positive side, the dragon and player both read clearly and the combat floor is open.
- **View 2:**
  - Spare towers and a separate gatehouse stand outside the arena on a bare grey slab, like leftover assets.
  - The arena block ends in empty space. A loose rock pillar stands off to the right.
  - The court is a plain square box with a flat floor. It is generous but bland.
  - All the murals glow like screens.
  - The dragon is tiny at this scale.
- **Views 3 to 6 (murals):** These are the best part of either version. There are four distinct subjects: a dragon above a marching army, a fallen crown with a lion banner, a chained dragon with a crest, and an astrolabe with a map of star patterns. All are aged and cracked, and the mortar joints show through. Their problems:
  - The same straight dark horizontal seam crosses every mural at mid-height (around y≈345–350 in each frame). It looks like a mesh or texture seam.
  - Iron studs sit on top of the paint, and the paint ignores the relief of the bricks, so the images look projected onto the wall, not painted on it.
  - Warm uplighting makes the murals the brightest thing in the scene. That produces the "bright display" look in views 1 and 2.
  - All four share one sepia illustration palette and look like digital prints.
  - In view 4, the painting is cut off abruptly at the left edge.
- **View 7 (main gate):** This is the strongest structure shot: good stone, iron bands with studs and lit arrow slits. However, the portcullis is flat black bars with no material detail. They look untextured.
- **View 8 (reverse view):**
  - Hard-edged, rectangular warm patches on the gate wall look like a lighting or shadow artifact.
  - The wall modules repeat visibly, and no base trim grounds the walls.
  - The floor is flat and bland.
  - The braziers are tiny for the space.

## 6. Evidence limits
- These are 8 still frames per version at 1280×720. I did not watch any motion.
- The close-up views have no player in frame, so I can't judge scale in them.
- The overviews (view 2) may show areas a player never sees in play. Even so, the leftover towers and void edges are real build defects.
- At matched camera positions, A and B show different surfaces, so the per-view comparisons in views 3–6 are not like-for-like.
- The target images are concept art. I used them only as a quality bar, not as a pass condition.
